#Splippy: Be The Flow!

A chain-clearing tile puzzler where you don't control the water — you ARE the water.
You are Splippy, a tiny water sprite hopping across a drought-stricken land. Every tile you activate brings the land back to life, every drop counts.

Tiles:
- Farm: Contains three crop types. Carrot, Wheat, and Corn.
- Ecosystem: Links with the previous crop type to boost the score.
- Gunk: Has a 2-turn decay; if left untreated, it turns into a landfill.
- Filth: Has a 1-turn decay; if ignored, it becomes a landfill and randomly spreads pollution to adjacent tiles.
- Landfill: An impassable tile. Splippy cannot move through it.
- Marine: When activated, releases a cross-shaped effect that clears landfill and both sanitation types along its row and column.

Controls:
- Click and drag to draw a path up to 7 tiles long
